# Route: reel-editor

- **Input:** One existing talking-head video (MP4 or similar) that should be edited into a vertical reel or ad.
- **Output:** A 9:16 MP4 with silences cut, word-timed Arabic captions, and motion graphics layered on, plus an `.srt`, a transcript `.txt`, and a cover frame. The footage itself is edited (cuts and zoom), unlike `/embedded-captions` and `/talking-head-recut`.
- **Triggers:** "منتج هذا المقطع", "شيّل السكتات", "حوّل الفيديو لريل", "ركّب كابشن عربي", "cut the silences and make this a reel".

## Interview

- **Must-haves:** which clip (the input file).
- **Deferred (announce):** the transcript review (which sentences to drop), the style and colors, and the scene design stay inside the workflow, where the transcript grounds them. Say they're coming.
- **Run-shape:** neither. On a phone the work runs in the background and the result returns in the same chat.
